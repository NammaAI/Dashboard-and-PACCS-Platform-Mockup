Use Case 02 · PACCS Platform · P1

Credit Decision Engine.

A PACCS secretary or DCCB officer enters a member ID. In under 90 seconds: complete member credit profile, loan eligibility against the Scale of Finance, recommended sanction amount, and a system-generated decision — with a full audit trail. What today takes 20 minutes of paper register lookup and produces a subjective output.

Worked example. Farmer Murugesan R., Member ID PACCS-0044-TJR-1187, walks in to renew his KCC crop loan. The platform retrieves his profile, applies the Scale of Finance, and presents a recommended decision — one screen, under 90 seconds.

What the platform changes. Today: a PACCS secretary spends 15–20 minutes checking paper registers, relies on personal memory of the farmer's history, and produces a handwritten recommendation with no audit trail. The Credit Decision Engine does this in under 90 seconds, applies the Scale of Finance consistently, documents every step, and surfaces credit risk signals the secretary may not have noticed. The eligibility rules remain exactly as set by the department — the platform enforces them, it does not replace them.

What this monitor enables

Today a district DCCB officer tracks each loss-making PACCS separately — different files, different visits, no ranked view, no intervention status, no early warning when a society is deteriorating despite active help.

This screen gives a single ranked view: severity order, current intervention, escalation flagged automatically when a society worsens despite active intervention.

Scale to all 38 districts: the department gets a state-level view of all 950 loss-making PACCS — which interventions are working, where to concentrate effort, and which societies need a different approach.

Connection to P1. The Credit Decision Engine and Society Health Monitor are two surfaces of the same platform. A society's health score is partly determined by the quality of credit decisions made at that society — prompt repayment rates, NPA levels, loan sizing relative to Scale of Finance. Better credit decisions today become better society health metrics tomorrow.

About this preview.

A working preview illustrating what a modern intelligence layer and operational platform over this department could look like. Read this before drawing conclusions about what is live versus what is illustrative.

What this preview is
Two use cases across eight screens. Use Case 01: five vertical dashboards (status signal + key metrics + decision items per vertical) and a unified convergence view. Use Case 02: two surfaces of the PACCS platform — a Credit Decision Engine for farmer-level loan eligibility, and a Society Health Monitor for the loss-making PACCS turnaround programme. Structure and logic reflect how a live system would work. Specific data within the screens is partly verbatim from Policy Note 2025–26 and partly illustrative of what a live system would surface.
What this preview is not
Not a prototype or live system. Screens are HTML mockups. No data pipeline exists, no CBS integration is in place. The farmer profile in the Credit Decision Engine (Murugesan R., Papanasam) is a constructed illustrative example showing the platform's logic — not a real record.
